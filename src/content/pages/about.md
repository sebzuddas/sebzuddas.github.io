# Hello! I’m Seb


##  I'm on a mission to build systems that **work**, and that have a potive impact on **people**. 

My heart lies in engineering, but I've always had a founder's impulse. The thread between the two is systems thinking, put to work building technology that solves real problems in people's lives.

That's what I spend my time on now. I'm co-founding a company I can't describe yet, and building a community to get ambitious engineers working on different things into the same room.

Before this, I worked as a systems and control engineer, mostly on systems where being wrong is expensive: state-space modelling, system identification, model predictive control, and the simulation rigs that prove a design before it meets hardware. At Spectral, energy assets and market access for energy traders and utilities. Before that, automation and predictive maintenance with Siemens across three cyber-physical factories, where a hybrid statistics-and-IoT method on live PLC data bought 10% uptime. Earlier, avionics for a two-stage sounding rocket with Project Sunride, modular vertical farming, and the agent-based modelling of the alcohol harm paradox that took best individual submission at Oxford's Map the System.

MEng in Systems and Control, University of Sheffield, and a Royal Academy of Engineering Leadership Awardee.

If you're building something hard and think I'd be useful, or you're an engineer who wants more of the interesting work, get in touch [here](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sebzuddas).
